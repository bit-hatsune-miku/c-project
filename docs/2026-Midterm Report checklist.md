---

**🎯 Purpose of This Assignment**

This presentation is designed to show that your team:

* Understands the **architecture of your program**

* Has made **real technical progress**

* Can explain your system **clearly and concisely**

A good presentation demonstrates **thinking and structure**, not just coding.

Remember:

If you cannot clearly explain your system, you probably do not fully understand it yet.

---

Your submission must include:

* All presentation materials (PPT, video, etc.)

* A prototype demo video

* Any supporting files used in the presentation

Upload the materials:

* **In the assignment submission**

* **AND in the Warzone forum**

---

**1️⃣ General Framework (System Architecture)**

You must clearly explain how your program is organized.

Your explanation should show:

✔ How **header files and source files interact**  
✔ How the program is divided into **modules**  
✔ How different parts of the system **communicate**

Good presentations usually include:

* a **system architecture diagram**

* a **module interaction diagram**

* a **program structure overview**

The audience should understand:

How the whole program works at a high level.

Avoid large blocks of code.

---

**2️⃣ Key Data Structures**

You must present the **most important data structures** used in your system.

Examples:

* core struct definitions

* scene representation

* dialogue storage

* state management structures

Explain:

✔ What each structure represents  
✔ Why it was designed this way  
✔ How it supports the game system

Focus on **design reasoning**, not syntax.

---

**3️⃣ Key Algorithms / Logic**

Explain the **core logic of your system**.

Examples may include:

* dialogue progression

* scene switching

* branching logic

* state management

* event handling

You should demonstrate:

✔ How the system behaves  
✔ How different modules interact during execution  
✔ How the game progresses from one state to another

Diagrams or pseudocode are encouraged.

---

**4️⃣ Current Prototype**

You must demonstrate the **current working prototype** of your game.

The prototype demo should show:

✔ Program startup  
✔ At least one functional gameplay sequence  
✔ Evidence of implemented systems

Use a **pre-recorded video** to demonstrate the prototype.

Running the game live during the presentation is **not recommended**, as technical problems may occur.

---

**5️⃣ Presentation Quality**

Your presentation should demonstrate:

✔ Clear structure  
✔ Logical flow  
✔ Effective visuals  
✔ Confident explanation

The presentation should follow this principle:

Show the system clearly instead of describing it abstractly.

Good presentations often include:

* diagrams

* screenshots

* short demo clips

Avoid:

* dense text slides

* long code listings

* unnecessary details

---

**6️⃣ Time Management**

Presentation length:

**4–5 minutes maximum**

Do **NOT exceed the time limit**.

Effective presentations:

✔ focus on key ideas  
✔ avoid unnecessary explanations  
✔ move quickly between sections

Students who exceed the time limit will likely lose impact.

---

**7️⃣ Preparation Quality**

Casual or improvised presentations are **not recommended**.

You should prepare:

✔ slides or visual materials  
✔ narration or speaking plan  
✔ demo video

Teams should rehearse before presenting.

A well-prepared presentation appears **smooth and confident**.

---

**8️⃣ Principles of Good Presentation**

Your presentation should follow three key principles:

**1\. Show, not tell**

Use diagrams, visuals, and demonstrations whenever possible.

**2\. Cut to the chase**

Avoid unnecessary explanation. Focus on what matters.

**3\. Save the best for last**

End your presentation with the **prototype demo**.

This ensures the presentation finishes with the strongest impression.

A weak presentation shows:

“Here is some code we wrote.”

A good presentation shows:
